### iPhone App of the Year finalists

* [AllTrails](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/alltrails-hike-bike-run/id405075943): An app that helps users navigate outdoor hiking, biking, running, and walking trails.
* [Duolingo](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/duolingo-language-lessons/id570060128): An app that guides users through learning over 40 languages with the help of a spunky, green owl.
* [Flighty](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flighty-live-flight-tracker/id1358823008): An app that offers radars, taxi times, commercial flight data, and more to travelers unsure about their [plane's whereabouts](https://www.zdnet.com/article/flying-soon-flighty-is-a-must-have-ios-app-for-air-travel/).
