### Method 2: Set up VPN on Chrome OS

 You can set up VPN in your Chromebook, which can take some time and patience since the VPN applications for Chrome OS doesn’t exist. So follow the steps below to set it up:

1. Subscribe to a VPN service. You can choose the one with good review on the Internet. If you don’t have the time or patience, try[**NordVPN**](https://tools.techidaily.com/drivereasy/download/) .
2. Click on the**user icon** at the bottom right corner in your Chromebook.
3. Click**Settings** .  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/img_5c32f534a583a.jpg)
4. Click**Add connection** under the**Internet connection** section, then select**Add OpenVPN / L2TP…** .
